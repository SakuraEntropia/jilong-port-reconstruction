- Caption: 吉隆口岸航拍画面; photographer 唐宋 / People's Daily Online. The article is dated 10 April 2023, reporting from 9 April. Exact exposure date is not separately given.
- View/use: genuinely elevated frontal drone view from the bridge side. Confirms the lower-hall footprint, embankment and facade hierarchy. Very little roof is visible, so it does not establish main building depth.
