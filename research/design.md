# 吉隆口岸国门 — reconstruction design

## Scope and identity
Create an editable, physically shaded Blender exterior scene of the Chinese gate / joint inspection building at the Jilong–Rasuwagadhi crossing. Use the documented 2019 southern, Nepal-facing appearance as the reconstruction baseline. The filename and scene metadata will explicitly identify this historical appearance. The port is a complex; this model concentrates on its recognizable gate, bridge approach and immediately surrounding mountain setting.

## Design choice
The best-supported approach is a proportion-led architectural reconstruction from dated photographs, with a complete three-dimensional exterior and openly labelled inferred depth. A survey reconstruction is unsupported because measured elevations are unavailable. An image projection would fail the editable geometry requirement. Therefore use actual mesh shells, recessed glazing, roof trim, a genuinely open road passage, and separate repeatable components.

## Art direction
The hero view looks north from the bridge, aligned closely with the 2019 source. Pale warm grey granite, dark blue-green glass, a tall rectangular portal, stepped Tibetan-style white dentils, four vertical curtain-wall bays per side and a red/gold emblem establish identity. A soft mountain daylight render, a three-quarter architectural view, an isolated rear inspection view and a dusk variation reveal material and geometry quality.

## Scene and performance
Model in metres. The main facade is provisionally 54 m wide and roughly 22 m high; these are proportional modelling values, not verified measurements. Separate collections for architecture, glazing, signs, bridge/site, vegetation, terrain, lights and cameras. Use linked meshes for repeated details and procedural PBR materials with physically scaled roughness and bump. Preserve editable bevel modifiers. The surroundings are approximate contextual geometry, not surveyed topography. No generated reference illustration is used.

## Completion criteria
The final .blend opens in Blender, contains nontrivial real 3D geometry, has a clear traversable rectangular passage, packed fonts/resources, named materials, multiple cameras, and successful rendered images. First inspect three blockout views, then inspect detailed previews and correct visible silhouette/material/composition defects before final renders. Record source confidence and geometry estimates, render settings, actual validation results and remaining limitations.
